Revenge is an Android music player for YouTube Music and the audio files stored on your phone.

Playback: background playback, a real queue, gapless transitions, crossfade, sleep timer, and speed control. Controls work from the notification, lock screen, headset buttons, and Android Auto.

Offline: download songs, albums, and playlists for listening without a connection. The local library scanner picks up the audio files you already have.

Lyrics: synchronized lyrics from LRCLIB, Paxsenix, YouLyPlus, BetterLyrics, KuGou, SimpMusic, and YouTube subtitles. Lyrics are cached on the device.

Sound: parametric equalizer with saved profiles, bass boost, stereo widening, and loudness normalization. USB DAC output is available for compatible hardware.

Discovery: home, search, charts, moods, artists, albums, and podcasts from the YouTube Music catalogue. Personalized recommendations are computed on the device.

Library: create and reorder playlists, like songs, view listening statistics, and resume the queue after a restart.

Connections: Last.fm scrobbling, ListenBrainz, Spotify playlist import, Listen Together group sessions, and Chromecast in the GMS build.

Appearance: Material 3 interface, dynamic color from album art, light, dark, and black themes, selectable app icons, home screen widgets, and animated canvas artwork.

Privacy: no account required. The FOSS build contains no Google Play services and no analytics SDK. Library, history, and settings stay in the app's private storage.
