Android music player, GPL-3

Your music library, on your terms.

Revenge plays YouTube Music and the audio files on your phone. No ads and no account. Downloads work offline, lyrics follow the song, and the equalizer is a real parametric one.

Get the latest release Read the source

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. FOSS and GMS builds are published separately.

01

One player for streams and files

Playback
Background playback with a proper queue, gapless transitions, crossfade, sleep timer, and speed control. Media controls work from the lock screen, notification, headset buttons, and Android Auto.
Offline
Download songs, albums, and playlists for offline listening. Files play from the local library with the tags you already have.
Lyrics
Synchronized lyrics from LRCLIB, Paxsenix, YouLyPlus, BetterLyrics, KuGou, SimpMusic, and YouTube subtitles. Translation is available through an endpoint you configure with your own key.
Sound
Parametric equalizer with saved profiles, bass boost, stereo widening, loudness normalization, and direct output to compatible USB DACs.
Discovery
Home, search, charts, moods, artists, albums, and podcasts powered by the YouTube Music catalogue. Recommendations are computed on the device.
Connections
Last.fm scrobbling, ListenBrainz, Spotify playlist import, Listen Together group sessions, and Chromecast in the GMS build.
Home screen
Widgets in several layouts, plus selectable app icons, dynamic color, and light, dark, and black themes.
Local first
Your library, history, playlists, and settings stay in the app's private storage. The FOSS build has no Google Play services and no analytics SDK.

03

Build it yourself

Revenge is GPL-3 software. The full source is on GitHub, including the Gradle build, CI workflows, and the modules that talk to YouTube Music, lyrics providers, and the audio pipeline. 

git clone https://github.com/tirthsaraiya29/Revenge.git
cd Revenge
echo "sdk.dir=/path/to/Android/Sdk" > local.properties
./gradlew :app:assembleUniversalFossDebug
